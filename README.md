FrostToast — a catalogue photograph of an impossible gadget: a toaster that toasts ice cubes. A cream two-slot toaster, shot as a clean studio product photo on a seamless white background, with two big ice cubes popping out of the slots. The absurd job reads at a glance: this toaster toasts ice.

The image is a composite of two real, freely-licensed Pexels photographs — a toaster with bread (Pexels 7937466, photo by Nicola Barts) and two ice cubes on white (Pexels 3675620) — combined by `tools/make_entry.py`, which cuts the ice cubes out of their photo and seats them in the toaster's slots. No AI image generation was used: the image model refused the request, so the entry is built from real photographs, honestly captioned here.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

This validates `entry.png` (whole PNG, at most 1 MiB and 1024 by 1024 pixels) and writes it to `/output/entry.png` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`).
